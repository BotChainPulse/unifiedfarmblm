Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Scope

Unifiedfarm BLM ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy covers the Unifiedfarm BLM website at unifiedfarmblm.com, related services, and our Android apps Farm Merge: 2048 Puzzle (package com.unifiedfarmblm.farmmerge) and PixelQuote (package com.unifiedfarmblm.pixelquote).

2. PixelQuote app data

PixelQuote does not require an account. Quote favorites, quote views, shares initiated in the app, daily progress, check-in/streak information, the currently selected quote, and cached trend settings are stored locally on your device. This information is used to provide offline features and to calculate the app's Trending and Rising lists.

PixelQuote's local engagement history is not uploaded to our trend feed. The app may periodically download a small public trend-configuration file from unifiedfarmblm.com to adjust timely category or keyword boosts. As with ordinary web requests, the hosting service may receive technical request information such as IP address, timestamp, and user-agent information.

You can remove PixelQuote's local data through Android app settings or by uninstalling the app, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

3. Farm Merge app data

Farm Merge does not require an account. It stores gameplay information such as the current board, score, best score and cached Premium status locally on your device so that gameplay can resume. The app checks Google Play purchase status when applicable to determine whether a Premium entitlement is active.

4. Advertising and consent

Our apps may use Google AdMob for advertising. Depending on your region and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostics and related signals to deliver, measure and protect advertising.

Where applicable, the apps use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent. A privacy-options control is shown when required so eligible users can review or change consent choices.

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and My Ad Center.

5. Google Play purchases

Farm Merge may offer a one-time Remove Ads purchase through Google Play. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment-card or banking details. PixelQuote currently does not require a paid account or subscription.

6. Information we do not request from these apps

PixelQuote and Farm Merge do not need access to your contacts, SMS messages, call logs, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location for their core features.

7. Website information

When you use our website or contact us, we may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery information or inquiry details. We may also receive ordinary website usage information such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and cookie or similar technology data.

  • Provide, maintain and improve our website and apps.
  • Keep PixelQuote favorites, streaks and engagement-based ranking available on the device.
  • Save Farm Merge progress and recognize valid Premium purchases.
  • Deliver and measure advertising where permitted.
  • Respond to inquiries and protect against fraud, abuse and security threats.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

9. Third-party services

Our apps and website may use services including Google Play, Google Mobile Ads/AdMob and Google's consent services. These providers process information under their own privacy policies and applicable legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

10. Retention and security

App data described as local remains on the device until cleared or uninstalled, subject to Android backup and restore behavior. Third-party providers retain information according to their policies. We use reasonable safeguards for information under our control, but no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or information about processing of personal data. Users in Uganda may also have rights under applicable Ugandan data-protection law. Advertising consent choices can be reviewed through the app privacy-options control where available.

12. Children

We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information through PixelQuote or Farm Merge. Advertising and target-audience settings are configured according to the selections made for distribution through Google Play and AdMob. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can review it.
