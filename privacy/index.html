Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. Scope

Unifiedfarm BLM ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy applies to the Unifiedfarm BLM website at unifiedfarmblm.com, our related services, and the Android application Farm Merge: 2048 Puzzle (package com.unifiedfarmblm.farmmerge).

2. Farm Merge app data

Farm Merge does not require an account. The game stores gameplay information such as the current board, score, best score and a cached Premium status locally on your device so that your game can resume. You can remove local app data through Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

We do not use Farm Merge to collect your contacts, messages, photos, microphone recordings, camera data or precise location.

3. Advertising and consent

Farm Merge uses Google AdMob to provide banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements. Depending on your region and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostics and related signals to deliver, measure and protect advertising.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where applicable to request and manage advertising consent. A Privacy choices option is available in the app so eligible users can review or change consent choices.

For information about Google's handling of data in advertising products, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and My Ad Center.

4. Google Play purchases

Farm Merge may offer an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase through Google Play. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment-card or banking details. The app checks Google Play purchase status to determine whether the Premium entitlement is active.

5. Website information

When you use our website or contact us, we may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery information or inquiry details. We may also receive ordinary website usage information such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and cookie or similar technology data.

6. How we use information

  • Provide and maintain the website, app and related services.
  • Save local Farm Merge game progress and recognize valid Premium purchases.
  • Display and measure advertising where permitted.
  • Respond to inquiries and process website orders or requests.
  • Protect against fraud, abuse and security threats.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

7. Third-party services

Our app and website may use third-party services, including Google Play, Google Mobile Ads/AdMob and Google's consent services. Those providers process data under their own privacy policies and applicable legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Data retention and security

Farm Merge gameplay state is stored locally until you clear the app's data or uninstall it, subject to Android backup and restore behaviour. Information handled by third-party providers is retained according to their policies. We use reasonable safeguards for information under our control, but no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or information about processing of personal data. Users in Uganda may also have rights under applicable Ugandan data-protection law. Advertising consent choices can be reviewed from the Farm Merge Privacy choices control where available.

10. Children

We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information through Farm Merge. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete it. Advertising and target-audience settings are configured in accordance with the selections made for distribution through Google Play and AdMob.
